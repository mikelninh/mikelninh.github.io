WHY THIS IS A WORKBENCH, NOT A CHAT CLONE

Keep your thinking
close to your doing.

This board organises links, your own notes and next actions. It cannot read ChatGPT conversations or embed their live interfaces. Open a chat from its card; use Chrome Split View for two real chats inside one browser window.

⌁ Your links stay local

Data is stored in this browser's storage for this site, not uploaded to a database. Anyone or any script with access to the same browser profile and website origin may be able to read it. Don't put passwords, private customer information or confidential chat content in notes.

TWO WAYS TO WORK SIDE BY SIDE

Actual chats, side by side.

01

Inside this dashboard

Paste a ChatGPT conversation link into any card. Edit next steps and notes without jumping between tabs.

02

Two real conversations in Chrome

Open two chats as Chrome tabs. Right-click a tab and choose Add tab to new split view. Chrome shows both ChatGPT interfaces side by side in one browser window.

03

More than two chats?

Keep 3–4 ChatGPT tabs in a dedicated browser window, or save their links in separate spaces here. A future browser extension could arrange existing tabs with your permission; this page cannot access them.

Optional local tab helper: not installed in this prototype.

Important: This webpage cannot access your existing tabs. ChatGPT doesn't provide these conversations to this independent dashboard, and browser framing protections prevent an embedded live chat view.
