03 / TELL THE TRUTH ABOUT IMPACT

Did something
actually change?

A click is not a donation. A promise is not a delivered bag of food. And neither proves how much suffering was prevented.

That is why Interbeing separates interest, self-reported action and independently confirmed outcomes. We are starting with the first two—not pretending we have the third.

This is a local prototype. Your responses stay in this browser. There are no shared visitor totals, server analytics or organisation-verified outcomes yet.

FIELDNOTES / SOURCES & LIMITS

Wonder, with
receipts.

The organisation's published descriptions ground the need and action routes. The person and animal in our journey are fictional illustrative figures, not beneficiaries we interviewed. The relationship map is explanatory, not a calibrated simulation.

Real impact evaluation requires the organisation's cooperation: for example, documented deliveries, recipients reached, and a careful assessment of outcomes—not a dashboard guessing lives saved.

A wider world exists beyond pet animals: farmed, wild and other potentially sentient beings matter too. This is one starting point, not a ranking of moral worth.
