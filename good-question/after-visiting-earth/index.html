A VIEW FROM SOMEWHERE ELSE
THE PREMISE

What would we miss most?

Imagine arriving home after a lifetime on Earth. What would you tell the ones who had never been?

Not the achievements or the things you owned. Perhaps the sound of rain on a window. The way someone said your name. A kitchen full of friends.

These are imagined letters from the other side of a human life — written for anyone still in the middle of theirs.
