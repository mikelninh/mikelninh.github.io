Instructions After Visiting Earth

What a soul might tell eternity, after experiencing an entire human life.

ABOUT 3 MINUTES TO READ

I have returned.
And I must tell you something.

Earth was nothing
like they described.

They warned me about suffering.
They warned me about forgetting.
They warned me that everything I loved
would eventually disappear.

They were right.

But nobody warned me
about the smell of rain.

Or how beautiful it feels
when someone laughs
so hard they cannot speak.

Nobody told me
that a simple meal
could become a celebration,

that a stranger could become family,

or that holding someone's hand
could make an entire universe
feel less lonely.

I made mistakes.
I hurt people.
I was afraid.

I spent years trying to discover
who I was,

only to find
that I was most myself
when I forgot about myself
and loved someone else.

And now that I am home,
I finally understand.

The extraordinary thing
about Earth
was never that life lasted forever.

It was that it didn't.

Every sunset was numbered.
Every embrace was temporary.
Every ordinary Tuesday
was a miracle
I almost didn't notice.

So if another soul
is preparing to go,

please tell them:

Don't spend your whole visit
trying to get back home.

For a little while,
Earth is home.

And it's beautiful.

A NOTE ON THIS LETTER

This is an imaginative poem, not a claim about life after death. Original writing developed by Michael Ninh with AI assistance for GOOD QUESTION. If it stays with you, send it to someone you'd love to share another ordinary Tuesday with.
