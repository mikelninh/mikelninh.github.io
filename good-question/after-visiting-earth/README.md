# AFTER VISITING EARTH — Vol. 01
A tiny original poetry collection inside GOOD QUESTION's Slow Edition. Built as a finite, accessible reading experience: no tracking, accounts, public submission forms, autoplay or fabricated subscriber metrics.

**Author/publisher:** Michael Ninh, with AI editorial assistance.

## Editorial
The letters are original imaginative poetry, not statements of scientific knowledge about death or the afterlife. Inspired by the general premise of instructions before human incarnation; all published lines are newly written, not reproduced from James McCrae's work. The book's name and author are not implied as contributors or endorsers.

## Reading and privacy
The Listen control uses the visitor's browser/device speech synthesis; no recorded voice is hosted. Personal reflections remain in browser localStorage and are not transmitted or published. Clear removes local saved notes. Copy and Share act on visitor request. JavaScript is optional for the poetry itself.

## Future pieces
Letters 002 and 003 are clearly marked in the making, not published. The site can be expanded by adding original stories and updating the RSS feed. EN is the original; translations only after editorial review.
