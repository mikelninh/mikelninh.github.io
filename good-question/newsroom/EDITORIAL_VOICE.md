# GOOD QUESTION / THE SLOW EDITION — EDITORIAL VOICE v1.0
Status: GOOD QUESTION is the existing publication home; the WUNDERPOST sub-brand remains provisional.
Date: 15 October 2026.
Mission: Stories about the extraordinary experience of being alive. Science, art, technology, philosophy, ethics, food and culture. 
Voice: Wonder with Substance.
Depth: deep and accessible, without pretension.
Humour: subtle, warm, rare, no punching down.
Narrative: collective exploration (“we”), no preaching or false consensus. Use I, you, or third person when the story genuinely requires it.
Ending: resonant image, observation or thought, not a generic motivational quote.
Rules:
1. Open with a real scene, observation or productive contradiction.
2. Earn depth through precise detail and honest reasoning.
3. Distinguish research, experience, interpretation and speculation.
4. Use human-scale examples; avoid overclaiming.
5. Vary sentence length naturally; avoid hollow one-sentence-paragraph cascades.
6. Make humour a quiet visitor, not a recurring punchline.
7. End when the thought lands. Do not explain the emotion.
8. Avoid formulaic intros, clickbait, pseudo-profundity and recycled self-help phrases.
9. Invite younger minds without dumbing down; explain ideas through concrete, safe examples.
10. Every feature must leave something worth thinking, feeling, discussing or trying.
Editorial standards: source non-obvious factual claims; distinguish medical advice; avoid unnecessary personal data; no unsupported wellness promises; offer alternatives to breath-focus for readers uncomfortable with it.
Distribution: website and newsletter are canonical; X for ideas and dialogue, Instagram for visual extracts, TikTok/Reels/Shorts for filmed hooks with a destination, YouTube for depth and trust. Repurpose intelligently, not by copying everything.
North star: stories readers voluntarily share with someone they care about.
