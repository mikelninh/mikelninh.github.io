# Partner & product philosophy (wishlist, not a partner announcement)

Our wish is to partner with people who make the world more interesting, compassionate and lovely — and who can tolerate honest criticism.

## Potential partner categories

- **Research:** ecologists and conservation scientists, animal ethicists, nutrition specialists, environmental journalists; first priority: an ecologist knowledgeable about lionfish control and a scholar of ahimsa. We would pay for expertise where possible, with clear contributor credit.
- **Creators:** Open Planet Studios (How to Live on Earth), yoga teachers, independent documentary filmmakers, illustrators, comics artists, musicians, game makers, nature photographers and tea growers.
- **Food:** independent Berlin vegan restaurants and grocers, plant-based makers including Alpro/Danone and Nongshim Germany/Kreyenhop. A possible collaboration is an expert interview or ingredient-label clarification; nothing agreed yet.
- **Distribution:** a transparent affiliate provider/network after approval, disclosure requirements, and valid links are established. We don't choose reviews based on fees.

## Product recommendation gates

1. Personally used and enjoyed? Declare a date, market and repeat-testing status. A founder saying 'I like it' is not a blind taste test.
2. Vegan ingredient classification? Check **exact SKU and country** and photograph pack; link primary maker info; verify allergens. Do not treat German Nongshim Shin marketing as proof about US or Toomba variants.
3. Compare apples with apples: flavour, price per amount, nutrition, purchase availability, packaging and value, dated evidence and limitations.
4. Tie tiers to transparent rubrics with sufficient first-hand testing; where lacking, show a 'To verify' category.
5. Affiliate/sponsorship relationships prominently labelled near the link. The editorial team retains final independence and the ability to give negative verdicts.
6. Retest changed formulas and remove dead links. Never imply retailers offer a fixed price when a source is a cached result.

## Partnership outreach template

Subject: GOOD QUESTION — an editorial conversation on [topic]

Hello, we're building a small independent magazine about food, ecology, care and curiosity. We're preparing a carefully sourced piece on [specific question], including perspectives that challenge our own. Would [expert/brand] be open to answering [two concrete questions] on the record, with an opportunity to check factual attribution? This isn't a request for endorsement or approval of our conclusions. We clearly disclose sponsorship and affiliate relationships where they exist. Here's our public editorial standard: [link]. Thank you for considering it.

**Never send without human review.**
