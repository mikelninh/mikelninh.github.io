# GOOD QUESTION — The Editorial Charter, v1.0

**Mission:** Help people live with more curiosity, care, joy, and less avoidable suffering. Our editorial identity includes plant-based food, animal ethics, ecology, yoga philosophy, tea, films, storytelling, original games, science and technology. This is an invitation to think, not an allegiance test.

**Words matter.** The Vegan Society's official definition of veganism is normative and extends beyond food: https://www.vegansociety.com/go-vegan/definition-veganism. We provide a faithful paraphrase plus the exact official link. The short original phrase “as far as is possible and practicable” is cited in context. Our editorial working language — reducing avoidable suffering and building good alternatives — is **not** a new official definition. Use 'plant-based' for diets, 'vegan' for the philosophy when explained, 'ahimsa' for a distinct tradition of non-harming, 'ecological management' for evidence-sensitive interventions.

## Core values

1. **Ahimsa with intellectual humility:** minimise avoidable harm to animals and people, without imagining a perfect universal calculation; respect the interests of individual animals even while studying ecosystems.
2. **Honesty with sources:** original publisher, exact claim, study setting/population, uncertainty and counterevidence; DOI or canonical URL; checked date. No synthetic percentages or implied formal GRADE assessment.
3. **Playful seriousness:** humour never substitutes for evidence and never humiliates sincere questioners. Immersive media and games illuminate a dilemma rather than pretending to solve it.
4. **Reader agency:** topic choices, finite feed, accessible reading, no dark patterns, reader-submitted questions and corrections.
5. **Commercial independence:** editorial rankings never depend on affiliate commission. Paid placements visibly labelled; partners get no review veto.
6. **Human accountability:** AI can research, translate, prototype, propose, edit and check. A human decides the story framing and release.
7. **Accessible optimism:** explain complexity in language people enjoy, never at the cost of accuracy. No perfection claims.

## Evidence states

- `original-reviewed`: original publisher/authority page read, relevant passage mapped, limits noted. **Not** independent peer-review of our summary.
- `secondary-only`: relies on a secondary account, flagged as such; do not publish strong claims without finding originals.
- `needs-verification`: do not state as settled fact.
- `opinion/ethics`: disclose editorial values rather than invent quantitative confidence.
- `professional-review-pending`: clinician, ecologist or other qualified reviewer not yet signed off.

## Disclosure on all editorial pieces

- Author credit: human publisher; when applicable 'AI-assisted editorial'.
- Research: primary links, access date, exact scope of review, key uncertainty, stronger opposing interpretations.
- Translation: AI-assisted until a native-language editor signs off.
- Products: personal favourite vs observed taste test vs manufacturer-declared label vs verified SKU; affiliate links if any clearly labelled.
- Corrections: what changed, why, evidence, date and editor, with link to original issue. Don't silently rewrite consequential claims.

## Sensitive subjects

Health and nutrition: avoid diagnosis, personal nutrition prescription, or overstating DGE positions; cite current DGE advice and qualified review. Ecology: interventions are context-specific, sometimes controversial, and should not be represented as zero-harm. Research participants, children, readers and third parties must not have private details exposed without consent. Interviews and photos require consent and licensing.

## Editorial workflow

`IDEA → PITCHED → RESEARCHING → SOURCES_CHECKED → DRAFT → COPY_EDIT → TRANSLATION_REVIEW → HUMAN_APPROVED → SCHEDULED → PUBLISHED → MONITOR/CORRECT`

The workflow can pause at any gate. AI-generated copy in `DRAFT` must not be displayed as expert-certified. Sources and facts cannot be assumed current from the last issue. Product formulas vary by market and date.

## Publishing cadence

Aiming for **four pages every fourteen days**, English-first Wunderpost and tri-language web publication where ready. Missing the date is acceptable; misleading readers isn't. Four great pages before eight mediocre ones. Weekly/fortnightly planning briefs can be automated as issues; public distribution remains approved and deliberate.
