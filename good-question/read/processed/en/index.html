06 / THE LABEL TEST

Vegan substitutes: brilliant or junk?

Processing is not a sin. A green label is not a free pass.

GOOD QUESTION · EditorialPublished 11 October 20262 minute read
LABEL
THE GOOD QUESTION PAPER / 002

We like convenient food. But ‘animal-free’ doesn't automatically mean ‘nutritionally excellent’. If the packaging shouts louder than the ingredient list, ask another question.

01 / THE OBJECTION

The strongest objection

Some meat alternatives are high in salt or saturated fat or low in fibre. Eating little besides heavily processed products can crowd out nutritious foods. That criticism is justified for some products.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

What the evidence actually says

The NHS explains that processing serves many purposes and not all processed foods are unhealthy; it recommends considering salt, sugar, saturated fat and overall quality. A 2025 French cohort study observed that the relationship between plant-based eating and cardiovascular risk varied with both nutritional quality and degree of processing. That challenges ‘vegan = healthy’, but does not establish that a given additive caused a disease.

A burger is not a diagnosis.

Processing describes what has happened to food; it does not, on its own, define the health effects of a whole diet. Tofu, fortified plant milk, bread and plant-based burgers are processed in different ways and have different nutritional profiles. Look at the full meal: how much protein and fibre does it contain? How much salt and saturated fat? Does a convenient item displace vegetables, legumes and whole grains, or does it make a more varied diet realistic for someone short on time? The observational cohort describes associations, not a definitive experiment establishing that processing independently causes harm. This is why both diet quality and degree of processing deserve attention.

03 / THE LIMITS

What remains uncertain

Observational research cannot remove every lifestyle confounder. NOVA processing categories do not capture all nutritional differences. A fortified convenient product may still have a useful place.

04 / YOUR NEXT STEP

A useful next step

Read the nutrition panel, not only the claims. Enjoy convenient alternatives when helpful, and build meals around beans, grains and vegetables.
