09 / THE OBJECTION THAT MATTERS

Don't animals die in crop harvesting too?

Yes. That is precisely why imaginary totals are unhelpful.

GOOD QUESTION · EditorialPublished 11 October 20262 minute read
✳
THE GOOD QUESTION PAPER / 002
?NO ROBUST GLOBAL DEATH COUNT
FISCHER & LAMEY · 2018

Mice, insects and other animals can be harmed by farming and harvesting. Hiding that destroys trust. Claiming a neat and exact death count for every diet goes too far as well.

01 / THE OBJECTION

The strongest objection

Growing crops needs land, machinery and sometimes pesticides. Animals can die directly or lose habitat. The ethical question is not ‘animal-free means harm-free’ but what harms different agricultural systems create overall.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

What the evidence actually says

Fischer and Lamey examine the problem in ‘Field Deaths in Plant Agriculture’. They stress that we know surprisingly little about its scale, and that even defining what counts as a death attributable to farming alters the estimate. Much cropland also produces feed for farmed animals, so comparative claims need to include that supply chain. Poore and Nemecek document major variation in farming systems and environmental impacts.

Beware of suspiciously precise death counts.

If someone presents a chart of “animals killed per million calories,” ask about the methods, species, time period and geographic scope before repeating it. Does it count only animals killed by machinery? Habitat loss? Insects? Indirect effects? The available literature gives reason to distrust neat universal figures. Crop production is also not simply the opposite of animal farming: crops may be grown as feed. That makes the accounting more demanding, not more convenient for either side. An ethically serious approach admits real harm from crop farming while refusing to present speculative numbers as proven verdicts about entire dietary patterns.

✳ What exactly was counted, over what area and time, and did the comparison include feed crops?
“Less harm is the aim. Zero harm is not an honest slogan.

03 / THE LIMITS

What remains uncertain

There is no dependable global ‘deaths per vegan plate’ figure that we can responsibly publish. Species, timeframes and indirect effects are difficult to compare.

04 / YOUR NEXT STEP

A useful next step

Support better data, less harmful pesticides, habitat-sensitive farming and lower resource demands. Be honest about remaining uncertainty.
