03 / FOOD FOR THOUGHT

But where do you get protein?

A sensible question deserving a better answer than ‘broccoli’.

GOOD QUESTION · EditorialPublished 11 October 20262 minute read
PLANT
THE GOOD QUESTION PAPER / 002
3812 WEEKS   /   PROTEIN-MATCHED TRIAL
HEVIA-LARRAÍN ET AL. · 2021

Protein is not automatically a problem — nor is it something to hand-wave away. Muscle growth requires sufficient protein, resistance training and recovery.

01 / THE OBJECTION

The strongest objection

Animal and plant proteins differ in amino-acid composition and digestibility. Some plant-based meals deliver less protein per serving; people with higher needs must plan more carefully. This matters particularly in sport and later life.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

What the evidence actually says

A 2021 trial followed 38 young men through twelve weeks of resistance training. The vegan group used soy protein supplements, the omnivorous group used whey; both averaged around 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. Researchers found no statistically significant between-group differences in the muscle and strength improvements they measured. This shows adequacy can work — not that all protein sources are interchangeable.

Protein adequacy is a whole-day question.

The small training trial did not compare an average vegan day with an average omnivorous day. Researchers matched protein intake and used protein supplements. That is simultaneously its strength and its limitation: it demonstrates that similar training adaptations are possible with adequately supplied protein from different diets, not that any random vegan menu delivers the same result. In everyday terms, total energy, the distribution of protein across meals and the choice of foods matter. Lentils, beans, tofu, tempeh and soy foods can contribute substantially; portion sizes must fit the person. An endurance athlete, a growing teenager and an older adult preserving muscle have different needs.

✳ What do my actual meals deliver across a full day, rather than what does one favourite ingredient contain?
“Ask not just plant or animal. Ask: how much, and for whom?

03 / THE LIMITS

What remains uncertain

Thirty-eight young men studied for twelve weeks cannot settle nutrition for every athlete or age group. Participants were habitual vegans or omnivores before the trial. Personal context still matters.

04 / YOUR NEXT STEP

A useful next step

Include beans, lentils, tofu, tempeh and other appropriate protein foods regularly. If you have demanding performance goals, plan intake with qualified support.
