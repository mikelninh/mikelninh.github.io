Status: **isolated previews only; current magazine unmodified**.
Built: 15 October 2026.
Compare: https://mikelninh.github.io/good-question/slow/design-lab/

## Concepts
- A / folio: expressive print-inspired magazine, cobalt and terracotta; confident art-led grid.
- B / atelier: warm cinematic, linen/olive; generous stillness, reading focus toggle.
- C / curio: tactile, curious and interactive; reader-controlled editorial exploration and optional decorative motion.

Each has a complete homepage and the *same original* long-form essay, copied from the existing publication to judge reading experience fairly. The existing stories in other sections remain where they are and are directly linked. Full existing magazine navigation, language controls, original routes, scripts and content remain unchanged.

### Not implemented / release blockers
- Concept selection and brand approval.
- Original cover art includes custom SVG illustrations; before a final release, commission or further refine image treatment as desired.
- Formal colour contrast measurements across states, full screen-reader audit and browser matrix required.
- Localised DE/VI typesetting for final system, including content translated and editor-reviewed.
- Social-share image assets and performance audits (mobile LCP/CLS); preview shares are textual.
- Automated QA for cross-page integrity could be added to the publishing workflow.

### Principles
Semantic markup, correct alt text and headings, skip links, visible focus, share controls, reading progress, responsive breakpoints and reduced-motion support. A, B, C previews live at separate URLs and use no analytics, forms, cookies or external APIs except Google Fonts; system font fallback applies.
