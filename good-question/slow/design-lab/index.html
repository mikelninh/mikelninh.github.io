WHAT WE'RE EVALUATING

Beautiful is only the beginning.

  • Distinctive, memorable editorial art direction
  • Excellent mobile reading and typography
  • Faithful content, sources and working links
  • Accessible contrast, focus and reduced motion
  • One coherent world across cover and feature
  • Interactions that genuinely enrich the story
