VEGAN /ˈviːɡən/

The Vegan Society describes a way of living that seeks, as far as reasonably possible in practice, to avoid animal exploitation and cruelty — beyond diet, not just avoiding meat.

Read the full official definition ↗
GOOD QUESTION / OUR WORKING LANGUAGE

Reduce avoidable suffering. Treat sentient lives with care. Stay honest about uncertainty and real-world trade-offs. Build alternatives people genuinely enjoy.

Our working philosophy is not an alternative official definition of veganism. We say “plant-based” when we mean food, and “reducing harm” when we're discussing a wider ethical aim.

OPEN STUDIO / BUILDING IN PUBLIC

The newsroom is part of the story.

We use AI to research, edit and build. The human publisher is responsible for every public claim. Here's our actual workflow — not a fantasy of an autonomous newsroom.

Q

QUILL

Editor-in-Chief

Plans the issue, prioritises the backlog and demands a clear reader benefit.

✳

PROOF

Research & verification

Checks original sources, exact claims, opposite evidence, variants and uncertainty.

▲

ATELIER

Design & interaction

Builds thoughtful reading, genuinely useful tools and little playable experiences.

♥

HUMAN

Publisher & final approval

Decides what is true enough, humane enough and useful enough to publish.

Agents can draft and check. They cannot silently publish health claims or invent interviews, product trials, partnerships or results.
