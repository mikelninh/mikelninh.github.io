01 / THE LETTER

Good things don't have to be complicated.

EDITORIAL · OCTOBER 2026 · HUMAN APPROVAL PENDING

A magazine can begin with a question. Ours began with several: how do we reduce needless suffering without becoming judgemental? Which foods make a kinder daily routine easier? Can a shared list of genuinely useful things be more honest than another advert disguised as a recommendation?

So here's a first letter from Mikel and Huong's kitchen. Not a polished shopping empire. Just the beginning of a careful ritual: we taste, we compare, we disagree, we change our minds. And before recommending anything as vegan, we check the actual ingredients in the actual market.

We're keeping our first public ratings empty until we each make them ourselves. You can try the tier list now and share a snapshot — the initials are editorial labels, not proof that a named person submitted a review.

03 / A LITTLE PAUSE

Tea, then one small decision.

Put the kettle on. Hold a warm cup. Look at what is already in your cupboard.

Pick just one thing to try this week — a recipe, an ingredient, or a question. You do not need to transform your life to make a thoughtful choice.

We like ideas from yoga and ahimsa, but this is a personal ritual, not a medical intervention or a promise of wellbeing.
