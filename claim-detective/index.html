01 / THE ONGOING WATCHER

One quiet scan. Repeated value.

The product is not a one-off chatbot. It is a recurring, permission-based investigation layer that notices changes, connects evidence and warns before money or rights quietly expire.

01 / INBOX + LETTERS

Notices and decisions.

Detect deadlines, missing documents, changed amounts, rejected requests and possible next steps inside selected messages and uploads.

02 / CONTRACTS + FEES

Recurring leakage.

Spot duplicate charges, price changes, forgotten subscriptions, unusual clauses and services that no longer match the agreement.

03 / BENEFITS + SUPPORT

Money left unclaimed.

Match household changes and life events to relevant public benefits, rebates and application windows without pretending eligibility is certain.

04 / CLAIMS + REFUNDS

Cases worth pursuing.

Prepare a structured case file with facts, evidence, unknowns, deadlines and a handoff to the right human or regulated partner.

03 / CASE FILE OUTPUT

From “something seems wrong” to a useful dossier.

Every finding should be understandable, inspectable and easy to hand over. The system helps organise a case; it does not impersonate a lawyer or hide uncertainty.

FACTS

What appears to have happened?

Dates, amounts, parties, documents, communications and the event sequence — each linked back to its source.

EVIDENCE + GAPS

What supports the claim?

Confirmed, partial and missing evidence states. Contradictions and ambiguous information remain visible.

NEXT ACTION

What should happen safely?

Collect a document, send a factual request, use an official process or hand the dossier to a licensed enforcement partner.

04 / OPEN + SUSTAINABLE

The rules can be open. The service can still be excellent.

The core should remain inspectable and copyable. People pay for trusted hosting, privacy, maintenance, integrations, recurring monitoring and professional handoff — not for being trapped inside a black box.

OPEN-SOURCE CORE

Public infrastructure for financial agency.

  • Claim and benefit rule packs
  • Evidence-state schemas
  • Document and deadline extractors
  • MCP tools and test fixtures
  • Evaluation sets and transparent limitations
  • Self-hosting for NGOs and public institutions
PAID HOSTED SERVICE

Convenience, vigilance and accountability.

  • Encrypted document vault and permissions
  • Monthly or event-triggered scans
  • Inbox, contract and account integrations
  • Family and small-business plans
  • Maintained rules and deadline alerts
  • Licensed partner handoff and success-based enforcement where lawful
A good detective does not create dependency. They make the hidden visible.

Claim Detective is a spiritual business in the practical sense: value before extraction, transparent uncertainty, no dark patterns, and success measured by how much agency people recover.

05 / BUILD THE FIRST REAL WATCH

Start narrow: one category, one partner, one measurable recovery loop.

The strongest first wedge is likely a document-and-deadline watcher for a clearly defined consumer claim, built with a regulated LegalTech or claims partner. The open platform grows only after the first loop reliably finds and resolves real value.
