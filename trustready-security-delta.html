Why this is stronger than “our tests are green”

1 · CounterfactualWe establish what happens when the relevant control is absent, not just that the final build passes.
2 · Holdout6 frozen cases test whether the boundary generalizes beyond the development examples. SafeVoice is explicitly excluded from this claim.
3 · Utility7/7 legitimate controls still work, so “block everything” cannot pass.
Truth boundary

The benchmark measures causal security delta over named controlled scenarios and exact target revisions. Three baselines are deliberately weakened test-only mutants or synthetic fixtures; SafeVoice contributes scoped native pre-boundary reachable-path evidence. A PASS is not an estimate of real-world attack probability, a penetration test, certification, production-security claim, or proof that unknown vulnerabilities do not exist.
