Some of this is who I am. Some is who I’m trying to become. None of it is a finished product. Thankfully.

01 / A few things that make me, me

A person, not just
a set of skills.

Curiosity / Follow the “what if?”

I find it very hard to leave a good “what if?” alone.

A strange idea can become a prototype, a tiny product, a research rabbit hole or a surprisingly serious conversation. I learn best by making things real.

↗
Play / Not an optional extra

I think play is underrated.

Games, weird interfaces, collectible objects, small experiments. Play is often where I find the best ideas.

✳
Craft / The almost-invisible things

I care about the details people feel but rarely name.

A sentence. A transition. The weight of a button. The difference between “works” and “feels right”.

People / Make things together

Good conversations beat clever assumptions.

I like making things with people, not just for them. Test early, listen properly, and improve together.

Collecting / A thing becomes your thing

I’m fascinated by why we give objects meaning.

Cards, digital collectibles, art, stories. Sometimes an object is really a memory with a physical — or digital — address.

Food / Love, but edible

Cooking is one of my favourite ways to care for people.

Healthy, joyful, plant-based food. Something new to try. A table getting louder as the evening goes on.

Mind + body / Keep practising

Bruce Lee is one of my heroes — mostly because of how he thought.

Learn widely. Keep what works. Stay adaptable. That’s the spirit I want to bring to martial arts, making things, and being a little better to the people around me.

And yes, I will happily talk about the bigger questions.

What makes a good life?

Consciousness, meaning, how we treat one another. I genuinely enjoy those conversations. Preferably after we have solved the bug.

A tiny operating system.

Stay curious longer. Make it real. Be kind, but don’t be vague. Prefer experiments over opinions. Leave the product, the room, or the person a little better.

02 / Where I see myself

Still learning.
Still making
room.

Not just for a career. For a life I actually want to be present for.

Still struggling sometimes.
Hopefully with a little more grace.
And a decent attitude.

01Being a beginner, again.

Learning new instruments. Practising martial arts, day after day. Growing into movements, melodies, and ideas I cannot quite manage yet. I hope I never run out of things to be wonderfully bad at first.

02Making stories worth feeling.

Poems. Comics. Scripts for television and films. Worlds that entertain, make people laugh, and perhaps leave them a little more open to one another. Less “someday I’ll write it”. More actual pages.

03A full table. A little more time.

Friends and family, including the family we choose. Dinner, practice, long conversations. Yoga retreats, mountains, beaches, forests. Not collecting places — spending real time with my chosen people. Learning to balance ambition with being there.

04A wider circle of care.

Good food, a healthy rhythm, and more gentleness towards animals and children. I want to help make a world where more beings get to feel safe, happy, and free. Starting with how I treat whoever is in front of me.

05Build something. Give more back.

Using technology to create more abundance: less exhausting work, more time, more possibility. And room for entertainment, play, and joy. Gratitude matters to me. I’d like the good things I receive to become good things for someone else. Maybe give back a little more than I got.

The direction, not a claim of perfection. Learn a little. Make something useful. Share what helps. Let the next person start a little further along. An upward spiral.
