Make room for a little more · four optional questions
Just take the free prompt
Help me write a warm, honest “Beyond the CV” introduction. Ask these five questions one at a time: What do I happily lose myself in? What am I still learning? What do I care about in ordinary life? What kind of future am I making room for? What would I love someone to ask me?

Invite concrete examples. Let me skip any question. Do not invent facts, turn hobbies into achievements, or turn aspirations into accomplishments. Do not ask for sensitive or identifying details. Use only what I choose to share.

Then draft a 120–180-word introduction in my voice, three conversation starters, and a short optional LinkedIn post. Keep it human, specific, and free of corporate clichés. Show me the draft for approval before treating anything as ready to publish.
