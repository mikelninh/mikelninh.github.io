      The reusable architecture

      The domain changes. The trust pattern does not.

      The point of the three cases is not that e-commerce, public administration and legal work are identical. It is that a consequential AI workflow needs the same basic controls wherever it runs.

      Source → Integrity → Evidence → Rule → Prepared action → Human decision → Audit

      AI does the repetitive work.

      Collect, reconcile, classify, retrieve, compare and prepare — the expensive busywork becomes machine-assisted.

      Evidence proves what happened.

      Important findings stay tied to a source version and exact locator instead of disappearing into an opaque answer.

      Humans keep consequential authority.

      Messages, case changes, approvals or other effects cross an explicit gate. Source drift closes that gate again.

      What this proves

      Not “trust the AI.”
      Inspect the work.

      These Golden Cases are deliberately honest about the current evidence boundary: they are portfolio simulations. The next level of proof is running the same admission criteria on authorized real-world cases and publishing the resulting metrics without upgrading a simulation into a production claim.

      Safety
