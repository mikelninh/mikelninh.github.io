THE SCIENCE / NO SUPERHUMAN CLAIMS

Extraordinary skills.
Ordinary honesty.

The method of loci, or memory palace technique, associates things with familiar places. Research suggests it can support recall for particular tasks. But results across studies vary and evidence quality is often low; a short before/after game can't establish a lasting change in memory.

Our first mission uses different word lists with location cues on the second round. It is a learning experience, not a controlled experiment. We show your real results even when your second score is lower. Read a 2025 systematic review ↗
