Working with me

Curiosity, kindness, courage — and useful weirdness.

I thrive in environments where curiosity, kindness, and courage are alive.

I work best when there is space to explore big ideas, turn them into simple systems, and build something useful for real people. I enjoy working at the intersection of technology, storytelling, ethics, design, and human wellbeing — especially when the goal is to reduce suffering, increase agency, and make complex things feel clear.

I bring the most value when I can move between vision and execution: sensing the deeper pattern, asking better questions, prototyping quickly, learning in public, and improving through feedback.

I care about humane systems, practical AI, consciousness, health, democracy, animals, and tools that help people live with more freedom and dignity.

The environment I thrive in is playful but serious about impact. High-trust. Mission-driven. Honest. Experimental. Warm. A little weird in the best way.

I am at my best when the work feels like service — and when the final question is not only “Does it work?” but also “Does it help?”
