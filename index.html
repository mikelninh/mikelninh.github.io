01 · Applied AI · Legal research

Finding the actual law should be the easy part.

GitLaw makes German federal laws searchable, with sources you can open and verify. The goal is a more useful starting point for research – not to replace legal judgement.

Built with retrieval, APIs, MCP tools and tests. Source lookup is stronger than end-to-end legal advice.

02 · A playful product · Made together

How good is that vegan snack, really?

GOOD THINGS is a little tasting shelf for vegan groceries. Rate what you’ve tried, drag products into S–F tiers, and compare notes with someone who may have wildly different taste.

Work in progress. We’re still improving the catalogue, product photos and nutrition details.

03 · AI Operations · Work sample

What happens when the system gets it wrong?

Claims Ops Loop is a small exercise around a synthetic insurance case. It lets you inspect a wrong decision, test a review rule and see the trade-off between speed and caution.

Eight fictional cases; deterministic checks. No live insurer data or model calls.

02 / How I work

My background is a mix of engineering, running a small business, product work and busy restaurant shifts. It’s taught me that the tricky part is rarely the idea alone. It’s all the exceptions, hand-offs and people who have to live with the result.

These days I work with Python, FastAPI, LLMs and agents. I like small releases, clear feedback, honest tests and asking someone to tell me what I’ve missed.
