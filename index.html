03 · How I work

Understand.
Build. Test.

I do not start with the model. I start with the person, the problem and what a good outcome should feel like.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the real problem.

Who is this for? What is hard today? What would make the experience genuinely better?

02 / CLARIFY

Make the decisions clear.

What do we know? What is missing? Which rules matter? What should a human decide?

03 / BUILD

Ship the smallest useful version.

Connect the data, AI and interface so someone can actually complete the task.

04 / TEST

Find what can go wrong.

Use real and simulated cases, clear limits and human checkpoints. Improve from evidence, not confidence.

04 · North star
Build environments where people can understand, act and thrive.

Rights, institutions, safety, money, food, culture, relationships and inner development all shape how much room a person has to live well. I want my work to make those systems more humane — while being clear about what technology can and cannot do.
