03 / WORKING METHOD

Investigate.
Build. Prove.

I do not begin with a model. I begin with the person, the workflow, the decision and the price of being wrong.

01 / INVESTIGATE

Understand the real case.

Who is affected? What is confusing? Which incentives and constraints shape the current process?

02 / STRUCTURE

Define the useful decision.

Turn messy inputs into explicit facts, rules, evidence states, unknowns and acceptance criteria.

03 / BUILD

Ship a vertical slice.

Connect data, backend, AI behaviour and interface so a real person can complete the workflow.

04 / PROVE

Expose failure before scale.

Add evals, citations, observability, limits and human checkpoints. Improve from evidence, not theatre.

04 / OPTIMISTIC MOONSHOTS

The futures I want us to reach soon.

These are deliberately optimistic targets, not cautious forecasts. Ambitious dates create urgency: they ask what science, policy, culture and technology would need to accomplish now.

2030 · GERMANY · EDUCATION

Meditation, Emotional Regulation and Self-Knowledge Become Core School Subjects

German schools teach attention, emotional literacy, conflict resolution and inner awareness alongside academic knowledge.

2035 · FOOD SYSTEMS

Global Meat Industry Completes Transition Beyond Animal Slaughter

Cultivated, fermented and plant-based food outcompete conventional livestock on taste, price and ethics.

2040 · BASIC SECURITY

Universal Basic Services End Extreme Material Poverty

Food, shelter, healthcare, education, energy and digital access become foundations of civilisation.

2050 · PEACE

Humanity Records Its First Year Without War

The breakthrough is not intelligence alone. It is coordination, restraint and institutions worthy of trust.

2055 · LONGEVITY

Last Ageing-Related Death Recorded—Voluntary Mortality Debate Intensifies

Longer life does not end the human question. It makes meaning, consent and the use of time more important.

2065 · CONTEMPLATIVE EDUCATION

Twenty-Year Silence Retreat Becomes a Leading Form of Higher Education

Once healthy lives become radically longer, sustained attention, direct experience and inner clarity become civilisation’s rarest skills.
