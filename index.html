01 / Direction

The future I want to help build

Useful AI should not merely make companies faster. It should help people understand, decide and act — especially when the surrounding system is complex.

Great technology should make people freer, healthier and more capable. I build systems that turn confusion into next steps, documents into decisions and fragile demos into reliable loops.

Curiosity, kindness, courage — and useful weirdness.

I move comfortably between vision and execution: understanding the deeper human problem, shaping a clear product, prototyping quickly and improving through feedback.

I enjoy the intersection of technology, storytelling, ethics, design and human wellbeing. I care about humane systems, health, democracy, animals and tools that increase agency.
