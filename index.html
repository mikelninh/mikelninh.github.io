03 · Working method

Investigate.
Build. Prove.

I do not start with the model. I start with the person, the workflow, the decision and the price of being wrong.

01 / INVESTIGATE

Understand the real case.

Who is affected? What is confusing? Which incentives, constraints and edge cases shape the current process?

02 / STRUCTURE

Make the decision explicit.

Turn messy inputs into facts, rules, evidence states, unknowns and acceptance criteria.

03 / BUILD

Ship the vertical slice.

Connect data, backend, AI behaviour and interface so a real person can complete the workflow.

04 / PROVE

Expose failure early.

Add evals, citations, observability, limits and human checkpoints. Improve from evidence, not theatre.

04 · North star
Build environments where people can understand, act and thrive.

Rights, institutions, safety, money, food, culture, relationships and inner development all shape how much room a person has to live well. I want my work to make those systems more humane — while staying rigorous about what technology can and cannot do.
