03 / WHY THIS MATTERS

The gulf between
an agreement and a life.

In the space between a signature and safety sit hundreds of choices about security, rights, institutions and accountability. Our job isn't to declare winners, but to make the evidence understandable and inspectable.

01

Find the actual document

Distinguish signed agreements, initialled texts, ceasefires, declarations and ongoing diplomatic processes. They do not carry the same legal meaning.

⌁
02

Track promises separately

For each commitment, define what would count as a real-world observation, and what is currently unverified or disputed.

◎
03

Inspect original reporting

Prefer dated UN, regional intergovernmental and independent reports. Distinguish statements from third-party verification.

↗
04

Revise when evidence changes

Publish what changed and why. A source watcher may detect new documents, but humans must evaluate their meaning.
