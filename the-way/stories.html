02 / Turning points

A promise is not a finish line.

24 NOV 2016

The revised final agreement

The Colombian government and FARC-EP signed the revised agreement. Its commitments extend beyond ending hostilities to governance, rural reform and victims' rights.

Agreement ↗
30 OCT 2026

Monitoring changes

The UN verification mission's mandate was renewed, with rural reform verification removed from its scope. The agreement itself still contains rural reform commitments.

UN mission announcement ↗
24 NOV 2026

Ten years on

The UN Secretary-General highlighted reintegration, territorial development and transitional justice, alongside continuing insecurity. The statement says five years remain in the implementation timeframe.

UN statement ↗

The human question

Can a family live safely, a former combatant reintegrate, and a displaced person return voluntarily and securely? These outcomes require separate evidence. The implementation percentage cannot answer them.

03 / SOUTH SUDAN · 2011–2026

A nation born. A peace still to build.

Independence in 2011 was a historic milestone. The revitalised 2018 agreement later created a framework for ending conflict and political transition. In November 2026, UNMISS reported that important benchmarks remained outstanding.
