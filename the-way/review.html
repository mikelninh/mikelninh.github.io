Machines investigate.

A bounded, scheduled research mission retrieves original public sources, verifies quotations and uses a small open-weight language model for a reader and a skeptic pass. Model judgments are not factual verification. Every candidate remains subject to human review.

Runs weekly on Monday; public-source evidence only. No account API key, automatic publication, or external political action.
