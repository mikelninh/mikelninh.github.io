THE METHOD · VERSION 0.1

No false certainty.
No forgotten lives.

01 / COVERAGE

53 theatre anchors ≠ all wars

The registry groups distinct wars, communal and criminal violence, plus dangerous tensions. One country may contain multiple theatres. UCDP's 65-conflict count is for state-based conflicts in 2025, not this atlas's total.

02 / GEOGRAPHY

Real geography, approximate points

Every pin uses geographic latitude and longitude for a region or conflict theatre, not the coordinates of a battle or a front line. Cross-border crises and displaced populations cannot be represented by one dot.

03 / TIMELINESS

Reviewed registry + live headlines

Conflict assessments are an editorial snapshot reviewed 23 November 2026. News reports refresh independently when feeds work; they never auto-reclassify a theatre. Dates and original sources must be checked before making decisions.

04 / PEACE OPTIONS

Hypotheses, not prescriptions

Each dossier gives possible components of a peace process, not a guaranteed solution, a negotiated offer, or support for one party. Affected communities, mediators, legal experts and local stakeholders must evaluate them.
