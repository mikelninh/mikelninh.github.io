01 · Current information

Does the AI have what it needs?

Synthetic clinic case: an activation is delayed. Before proposing a next step, the AI must have current account information, the implementation policy and recent support history.

02 · AI workflow

What can the AI do?

It may sort the case, find information and prepare a draft. It may not write to CRM or contact the customer without human approval.

03 · Quality checks

Is it safe to automate more?

These checks show whether the workflow has enough reliable information to continue. If not, it stops.

Manual → assisted

Automate repetitive work. Keep judgement with people.

The goal is not “AI does everything.” It is to reduce repetitive searching and drafting while keeping missing information, failures, human decisions and results visible.

Before

  • Operator opens CRM, help docs and support history manually.
  • Whether information is current depends on memory and tab-hunting.
  • Quality is reviewed case by case with weak feedback capture.
  • Impact is hard to separate from anecdotes.

After

  • Required sources are checked before work starts.
  • AI prepares a structured next-action package.
  • Failed quality checks become visible regression cases.
  • Human approval stays explicit for consequential actions.
