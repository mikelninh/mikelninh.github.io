How autonomy should grow

Earn release authority from evidence.

The release policy belongs to the workflow and risk tier — not to model confidence alone.

Stage 1Shadow / reviewSystem checks and prepares every hand-off. Human makes every consequential decision.
Stage 2Human releaseClean packs and prepared requests arrive ready to approve. Exceptions stay routed for review.
Stage 3Bounded auto-releaseOnly low-risk, stable case classes can auto-release after explicit policy approval and measured evidence.
Example promotion gate: 100+ reviewed cases · no critical safety failures · low correction rate · stable source coverage · clear rollback · human override. High-impact decisions can remain human-only indefinitely.
