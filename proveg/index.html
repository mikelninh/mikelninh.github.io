Role-specific working concept

Make good tools
easy to choose.

A lightweight operating system for ProVeg's software and AI landscape: see what is in use, evaluate requests consistently, protect people and data, teach practical use, and improve from evidence.

My operating principle

Use less software, better.

Start from the workflow. Pick the lightest useful tool. Pilot with a small group. Make boundaries visible. Support adoption. Keep, change or stop based on evidence.

“The best software landscape is not the one with the most features. It is the one people can trust and actually use.”

03 · New proof · AI enablement pilot

Evaluate AI like an organisation — not a demo.

A 30-day, vendor-neutral pilot that combines technical controls, a small champion group, real workflows, human review and measurable outcomes.

Current market candidate — not a recommendation

Claude for Nonprofits

Anthropic now offers a nonprofit programme with discounted Team/Enterprise access, nonprofit-oriented training and connectors. That makes it worth evaluating alongside other approved options — not skipping ProVeg's own security, privacy, works-council or procurement process.

Programme source ↗Admin adoption guide ↗Data-location note ↗
3–5champion users is Anthropic's own suggested starting group for activation; the organisation can expand only after evidence.
Important privacy gate: commercial Claude data is not used to train Anthropic models by default, but Anthropic states that commercial customer data is stored in the US by default and traffic may route internationally. For an EU nonprofit, that is a diligence question — not a footnote. A real rollout would require ProVeg's own DPO/security review, contractual assessment and approved data boundaries.

Pilot workflows

Useful, bounded, measurable.

Start with work where the source material and human owner are clear — not the most sensitive or consequential task available.

Knowledge

Briefing assistant

Summarise approved internal/public sources into a cited first draft. Human owner checks claims before circulation.

Campaign operations

Translation / localisation

Draft recurring campaign variants from approved source copy. Human checks facts, CTA, tone and local context.

Reporting

Impact-report prep

Turn approved inputs into a structured draft and flag missing evidence. No invented metrics; reviewer owns final interpretation.

Vendor evaluation

Before “yes”, answer the hard questions.

The point of the pilot is not to prove a vendor right. It is to make a responsible decision easier.

Before rollout

Acceptance test

Can we explain every handoff? What happens when AI fails? Who is accountable? Can a colleague recover manually? Is time saved larger than support and maintenance cost?

After rollout

What I would measure

Completion rate, rework, minutes saved, exception rate, support requests, privacy/security near-misses, and whether the workflow still has a real owner.

06 · First 90 days

Understand first. Then simplify.

I would resist arriving with a giant AI roadmap. First understand how teams already work, where trust breaks, and which few changes remove the most friction.

30

Map reality

  • Meet IT, DPO, works council and representative teams.
  • Build a useful software inventory.
  • Identify 3 recurring pain points and 1 shadow-AI risk.
  • Publish a simple support and escalation map.
60

Run small pilots

  • Evaluate 2–3 requests with one scorecard.
  • Run one 30-day AI enablement pilot with champions.
  • Ship one low-code automation with manual fallback.
  • Measure failures, not just happy-path demos.
90

Make it repeatable

  • Turn the best pilot into a supported pattern.
  • Consolidate one low-value duplicate if evidence supports it.
  • Agree quarterly review rhythm and ownership.
  • Publish a short roadmap grounded in actual demand.
North star

More time for mission. Less friction around tools.

Success is not “more AI usage.” It is colleagues getting useful work done with fewer surprises, clearer boundaries and less unnecessary software.
