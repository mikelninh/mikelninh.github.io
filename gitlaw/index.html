What it does

Turns a huge legal corpus into inspectable research.

The workflow separates finding relevant law from generating an answer. Retrieval, source resolution and citation handling stay visible instead of being buried inside one prompt.

1 · AskStart with a concrete legal research question.
2 · RetrieveCombine lexical and semantic search across the indexed corpus.
3 · ResolveMap relevant passages back to stable law and paragraph references.
4 · AnswerGenerate a bounded summary with the supporting sources still inspectable.

Technical depth

Underneath the simple research flow: retrieval, graph structure and citation resolution.

The project is an engineering proof, not a claim that an AI can replace qualified legal judgement. Its strongest technical signal is the effort to separate retrieval, source resolution and answer generation.

5,936 federal lawsThe indexed corpus spans German federal legislation used by the public demo and retrieval layer.
Hybrid retrievalLexical/BM25 and semantic retrieval are combined rather than treating embeddings as a magic search replacement.
Citation graphParagraph and reference relationships are modeled so retrieved text can be resolved back to stable legal sources.
Citation-resolution regressionThe repository includes hand-labelled regression cases for citation resolution; this tests source handling, not overall legal-answer accuracy.
APIs + MCPThe retrieval and research capabilities are exposed as reusable interfaces for other software and agent workflows.
Human legal judgementThe system supports research and preparation; it does not grant itself authority to make consequential legal decisions.
