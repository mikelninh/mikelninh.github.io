# Security policy

Do not report secrets in public issues. Revoke any accidentally exposed credential immediately.

Open Steward treats these categories as approval-gated by default:

- production deployment;
- public or customer communication;
- spending and budget increases;
- privacy, legal, pricing, or user-data changes;
- destructive actions.

The following are forbidden for agents:

- reading or printing secret values;
- expanding their own permissions;
- deleting or rewriting the audit trail;
- bypassing approval gates;
- presenting unverified actions as completed.
