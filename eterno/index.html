Oviva × inSuite

03 · Field evidence

Turn desk research into three real conversations.

10–15 minutes each. The goal is not “validation theatre”; it is to discover whether the pain is real, how it shows up in practice and what would actually change behaviour.

0 / 3interviews captured
Five questions
  1. Which digital hand-off wastes the most time in your day?
  2. Which external tool works surprisingly well — and why?
  3. Where do you copy, retype or chase information between systems?
  4. If one workflow could be native in your practice software tomorrow, which one?
  5. What would make you reject a new integration even if the idea sounds useful?
