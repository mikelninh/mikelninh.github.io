# 6-Slide Capstone Presentation

## Slide 1 — Recommendation
One sentence: what I would do and why.

## Slide 2 — Current → Target Flow
The patient / clinic journey and key technical boundaries.

## Slide 3 — Implementation Plan
Sequence, owners, dependencies and timing.

## Slide 4 — Risks + Mitigations
Top risks, blast radius, fallback / rollback.

## Slide 5 — Validation
Acceptance tests, evidence and go/no-go criteria.

## Slide 6 — Go-Live + Monitoring
Launch scope, first-hour/day checks, ownership and next iteration.

## Hiring-manager challenge questions
- What evidence would change your recommendation?
- What would you do first if time were cut in half?
- Which system do you deliberately *not* touch, and why?
- What does a safe fallback look like?
- How do you explain this to a non-technical practice manager?
