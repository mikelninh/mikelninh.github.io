Broken Calls · flagship vertical slice

The ideal first run.

08:47Mina: “Some calls are disappearing. The team thinks your AI is down again.” Four patients wait outside.
TURN 1TALK → scope. Location B only. Location A becomes visibly healthy.
TURN 2TALK → what changed? Telecom provider changed yesterday.
TURN 3LOOK → assistant logs. Failed B calls never reach ingress. AHA: failure is upstream.
TURN 4Contain: route B directly to staff. Queue shrinks; trust rises.
TURN 5LOOK → forwarding config. Old provider destination remains. Routing room wobbles.
TURN 6TRY → targeted A/B call. Signal physically stops at routing; after fix it completes.
CLEARMission Clear ★★★★. Explain scope, evidence, containment, root cause, fix and monitoring.
Sound

Cozy competence.

Soft taps, paper shuffles, tiny cable switches, bright clue plinks, a 3-note AHA rise and a warm four-note clear chord. No alarms unless absolutely necessary.

Scoring

Score behaviour, not collection.

Diagnosis — did the explanation fit the evidence?
Safety — was the blast radius contained proportionately?
Efficiency — how much irrelevant work happened?
Communication — did the clinic stay informed and confident?

Originality

Console-grade clarity, not imitation.

The aspiration is tactile joy, strong readability and approachable depth. We do not copy Nintendo characters, UI layouts, sound motifs or recognizable art styles. Our own identity is miniature healthcare operations + detective notebook + warm technical systems.
