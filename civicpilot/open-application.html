Why I cannot stop thinking about it

Public institutions are full of workflows where citizens wait, staff repeat work, and small process failures become real human frustration. AI should not replace accountability there. It should make useful service easier: clearer forms, better routing, faster checks, and more time for humans to handle the difficult cases.

Problem

Government teams need help turning vague AI interest into safe, measurable pilots.
