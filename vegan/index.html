01 / WHY VEGAN

A philosophy with everyday consequences.

For me, veganism is not an identity badge. It is a practical attempt to align food, consumption and product choices with compassion and planetary health.

01 · COMPASSION

Reduce avoidable harm.

Animals are sentient lives, not invisible inputs. I want my choices to cause less suffering where a realistic alternative exists.

02 · HEALTH

Food first. Evidence matters.

A vegan diet should support a strong life, not just satisfy a label. That means reliable B12, solid protein foods, sensible supplementation and honesty about uncertainty.

03 · JOY

Make the better choice desirable.

Taste, convenience, design and culture matter. A compassionate future becomes more likely when the food is genuinely delicious and the products are fun to share.

THE BROADER IDEA

Compassion is not softness. It is a design constraint.

The interesting question is not only whether a product works. It is whether the system behind it helps humans, animals and the planet flourish together.
