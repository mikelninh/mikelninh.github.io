How are results scored?

Eight field statuses per case: found, missing, different, forecast or mixed. We measure exact status agreement against 48 hand-labelled results, plus grounded quotes and measured API latency. A matching label is not proof that a founder's claim is true.

Where are the difficult cases?

Four cases already used during development and two deliberately harder synthetic challenge cases. The challenging documents use unusual wording and separated forecast context. Even these are not independently sampled real-world decks.
