02 / WHAT PEOPLE GET WRONG ABOUT AI

The model is not the system.

People overfocus on model intelligence and underfocus on the system around it. The interesting part is where AI meets tools, data, feedback, permissions, people and real-world consequences; a slightly weaker model inside a well-designed workflow can be much more valuable than a brilliant model inside a demo. More agentic AI makes evaluation and responsibility more important, not less.

03 / TECHNICAL LEADERSHIP

End-to-end ownership, without pretending.

On GitLaw, I owned technical direction from ingestion through retrieval, API contracts, frontend workflows, MCP integration, evaluation and deployment. I decided where deterministic verification mattered more than another model call, how evidence should travel through the stack and what had to be tested before users could trust it. It was not leadership of a large enterprise engineering team; it was genuine ownership of a substantial full-stack product.
