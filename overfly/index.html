03 / THE MESSY BRIEF CHALLENGE

“We have thousands of documents. Can we do something with AI?”

My first contribution is not choosing a framework. It is turning the vague request into a measurable product question and a thin end-to-end experiment.

01 / OBSERVE

Who does what today?

Map the real workflow, hand-offs, exceptions, incentives and the expensive moments of uncertainty.

02 / DEFINE

Which decision improves?

Name the user outcome, acceptable error, required evidence, latency, cost and human responsibility.

03 / SHIP

Build the thin slice.

One document type, one decision, one interface and one complete route through the system.

04 / PROVE

Attack the promise.

Golden cases, adversarial inputs, explicit failures, user feedback and metrics that determine whether to continue.

STRONGEST NOW

From ambiguity to a working product slice.

Product discovery, applied-AI workflows, Python backends, retrieval, MCP, structured outputs, evaluations, guardrails and interfaces that make the system usable.
