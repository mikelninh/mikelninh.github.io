The three prompts

What I would send you.

Overfly’s open application rewards clarity more than a ceremonial cover letter. These are my direct answers.

01 / PROJECT

GitLaw — because confident AI is not enough.

Laws and official processes shape people’s lives, yet the information is difficult to navigate and AI can create dangerous certainty when it is wrong. I built a live RAG and MCP platform over 5,936 German federal laws with hybrid search, local citation verification and 53/53 passing citation evals. I cannot stop thinking about the broader pattern: making complex systems understandable without hiding uncertainty or human responsibility.

02 / CURIOSITY

You live between possible and useful.

Your path from high-impact use-case discovery through product and engineering to production fits how I want to work. The open application also signals that you value people who bring a useful combination rather than matching a rigid title — which suits my mix of engineering, product, founder and operations experience.

03 / FIRST MOVE

An Applied AI Reliability Kit.

I would start with a lightweight shared kit for faster client delivery: use-case canvas, typed skill contract, golden-set harness, guardrail patterns, cost and latency tracking, decision receipts and a clean handover pack. I have already built the pieces across GitLaw, SafeVoice, Agent Loop Lab and a reusable MCP toolkit.

First contribution

Useful speed, not a heavy framework.

The kit would exist to answer four questions early: Is it useful? Is it grounded? Does it recover? Can the client inherit it?

Use-case canvas

User, decision, current cost, evidence, action, risk and measurable success.

Typed skill contract

Inputs, outputs, permissions, failure states and human checkpoint.

Golden-set harness

Representative, adversarial and edge cases with deterministic assertions first.

Operational rails

Prompt/model version, latency, cost, retries, idempotency and tool logs.

Decision receipt

Evidence, assumptions, uncertainty and action in one inspectable record.

Handover pack

Architecture decision, runbook, eval set, limits and next experiments.
