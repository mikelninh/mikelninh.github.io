03 / Application answers

The three questions, plainly answered.

No treasure hunt, no accordion and no generic cover-letter fog. These are the answers I would send in the email — with the projects above available for inspection.

01

A project you are passionate about: what is it and why can’t you stop thinking about it?

The project I keep returning to is GitLaw: a legal AI system grounded in 5,936 German federal laws, built to give understandable answers with verifiable sources rather than confident guesses. It combines retrieval, structured outputs and citation evaluations, including 53/53 passed citation test cases.

I cannot stop thinking about it because access to rights often depends on whether someone has enough time, money or expertise to navigate complexity. GitLaw is one expression of a broader question that motivates me: how can AI make essential systems genuinely easier to access?

02

What made you curious about Overfly?

Overfly sits exactly at the intersection I want to work in: understanding a real operational problem, identifying where AI creates meaningful value and then building the solution into a usable product. I like that you combine strategy, product thinking and hands-on engineering rather than treating AI as a detached research exercise or a flashy demo.

Your small, entrepreneurial team also feels like an environment where I could contribute broadly, learn quickly and take real ownership.

03

What would you want to tackle first if you joined us?

I would start with one real client workflow and map the full path from the current pain point to a measurable outcome before choosing the technology. Then I would build a thin end-to-end version with structured outputs, evaluations, explicit failure cases and early user feedback.

Once it works, I would help turn the reusable parts — evaluation patterns, agent components and implementation learnings — into building blocks that make the next Overfly project faster and more reliable.
