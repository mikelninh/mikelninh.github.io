01 / HONEST STATE

Does it work?

The rules and game loop are real. The product is not yet publicly deployed or proven fun with a returning community.

YESMechanically playable

Solo play against three bots, Northern and Southern variants, legal-move enumeration, results and rematches.

YES, LOCALLYMultiplayer architecture

Private PartyKit rooms, authoritative state, redacted hands, bot fill, chat, avatars, reactions and Bốc.

NOT PROVEN YETFun and retention

Real four-player sessions, repeated rematches and voluntary return behaviour still need to be tested.

02 / WHAT EXISTS

A serious prototype.

The current build explores game systems, real-time engineering, social expression and ethical cosmetic monetisation.

RULES

Deterministic game engine

  • Northern and Southern configurations
  • Singles, pairs, triples, straights and bombs
  • Exact-suit Northern triple rule
  • Hundreds of simulated full games
SOCIAL TABLE

Private play with personality

  • Shareable private rooms and room codes
  • Selectable avatars, live chat and reactions
  • Animated Bốc lead ceremony
  • Disconnect bot takeover
EXPRESSION

Cosmetics, never power

  • Card backs, faces and table themes
  • Local prototype wallet and collection flow
  • No betting, loot boxes or stat advantages
  • Jade Dynasty visual prototype
LANGUAGES

English today

The current interface is English-only. German and Vietnamese localisation are roadmap items, not finished features.

03 / ROADMAP

What earns the next version?

The largest remaining risk is not whether the rules execute. It is whether real people enjoy the table enough to return.

NEXT
Four-player playtests

Test Bốc pacing, rule disputes, mobile readability, chat, laughter, frustration and rematch behaviour.

THEN
Language and accessibility

Add Vietnamese and German, keyboard support, reduced motion, sound controls and clearer mobile touch targets.

BEFORE PUBLIC
Reliability and safety

Reconnect to original seats, persistence, moderation, blocking, reporting, rate limits and privacy decisions.

ONLY IF EARNED
Progression and production art

Accounts, fair progression and authored collections only after people genuinely want to keep playing.

A beautiful game nobody returns to is still a failed product.

The next milestone is behavioural evidence—not another feature list. Real tables should decide whether this deserves to become a public game.
