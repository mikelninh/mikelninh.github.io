How we decide what deserves to scale

Observe → Act → Measure → Learn

Define the baseline, run a bounded experiment, record correctness, human effort and unintended harm. Prefer independent review. A failed gate means improve or stop — not hide the failure.

INCA example: the first 30 newly authored synthetic cancellation cases produced 24 matching routes and two unsafe continuations. Inspect the recorded results. No insurance expert review or operator-time measurements yet; release blocked.
