Earned autonomy
Why not approve forever after enough clean cases?
That can be the right direction — but autonomy should be promoted by evidence and scope, not by a magic click count. Repeated approvals are a signal; outcome quality and failure behaviour are the gate.
Shadow prepare only; every case reviewed
Copilot human approves every consequential action
Limited auto auto-release only low-risk, well-bounded cases
Trusted broader authority only after sustained evidence
Illustrative promotion evidence: 100+ reviewed cases · near-zero material corrections · 0 unsafe/false-completion events · stable source coverage · explicit rollback and monitoring. Promotion still requires an intentional policy change.
