The important bit

You should never have to guess what is still open.

CareOS keeps pending, preliminary, corrected, unavailable and final information visibly different. An AI explanation may change the wording — never the underlying source state.

Safety rule

Pending ≠ negative.

If the source is down or a result is not finished, CareOS must never turn that into “nothing found.” Uncertainty remains visible until the source resolves it.

Source truth

Plain language never replaces the record.

Every important explanation should lead back to the original source, state and timestamp where the connected system exposes them.

Family / proxy access

Delegation, not shared passwords.

A future family/proxy view should use explicit, revocable scopes. Who may see or share what remains governed by the connected health infrastructure and applicable policy.

Agency

Patients should be able to flag possible errors.

Flagging does not silently overwrite the clinical record. It creates a visible review request with ownership and resolution state.

Comprehension over engagement

Can the patient explain the plan back?

Our usability proof should test understanding, not page views.
