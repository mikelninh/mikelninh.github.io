Golden synthetic journey · one patient end to end

Useful when everything works. Safe when something doesn't.

One synthetic episode connects the product philosophy: trustworthy source context, a bounded clinician draft, a corrected result during an outage, recovery that reopens stale work, patient understanding and an acknowledged follow-up beyond the hospital.

Synthetic/pre-hospital only. This is a regression story and product specification — not clinical validation, a live integration or a measured outcome.
