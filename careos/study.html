Evidence rules
1. Paired matched cases. Each participant sees baseline and CareOS with different comparable synthetic variants.
2. Counterbalanced order. Both baseline-first and CareOS-first participants must appear in a highlighted set.
3. Safety beats speed. Wrong patient, missed pending work, unsupported/stale claims, draft-as-recommendation or verification collapse block a result.
4. Minimum evidence. No directional role/workflow result before ≥5 complete safe pairs.
5. No clinical-efficacy leap. A faster synthetic workflow only earns the next research question.

The machine-readable contract and aggregation logic live in app/time_returned_to_care.py and scripts/aggregate_time_returned_to_care.py.
