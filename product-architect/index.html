How I build

This is the work.

I want a reviewer, customer or teammate to be able to follow the chain from the original problem all the way to evidence. No mysterious “AI magic” in the middle.

I found this problem.Start with the actual friction, not a technology looking for somewhere to land.
Here’s why it matters.User pain, cost, risk, missed opportunity or public value.
Here’s what I decided to build.A deliberate product boundary — including what I chose not to build.
Here’s the architecture.Data, APIs, capabilities, state, permissions and trust boundaries.
Here’s why I made those trade-offs.Reversible choices move quickly. Hard-to-reverse choices earn more thought.
Here are the dangerous failure modes.What happens when retrieval fails, evidence conflicts, permissions are wrong or the model sounds certain when it should not.
Here are the agents I used.Spec, architecture, build, eval, trust, UX and deployment — with clear responsibility boundaries.
Try the actual product.A working increment beats a beautiful architecture diagram that cannot be touched.
Here are the three tests that matter.Golden cases protect the highest-value workflows instead of rewarding feature count.
Here are the results.What passed, what failed, what a user gained and what the evidence really supports.
Here are the remaining gaps.Unknowns stay visible. A green build never upgrades an unproven real-world claim.

Standard contract

Six files. Every serious product.

The Product Architecture Pack is intentionally small. It gives humans and agents a shared contract without turning product work into document maintenance.

01

Intent

User, painful job, desired outcome, non-goals and the reason this deserves to exist.

02

Product spec

Core workflows, user-visible behaviour, failure states and acceptance criteria.

03

Architecture

Data, APIs, system boundaries, capabilities, trust boundaries and hard-to-reverse decisions.

04

Constraints

Security, privacy, authority, compliance, performance, cost and claim boundaries.

05

Golden cases

Exactly three flagship workflows with realistic inputs, failure conditions and proof requirements.

06

Verification

Tests, E2E, pilot evidence, known gaps and the bar for the next proof level.

How it stays alive

A change that alters product behaviour, a trust boundary, a hard-to-reverse architecture choice, a golden case or the evidence behind a public claim updates the relevant pack in the same change. Implementation details inside the contract do not create documentation churn.
