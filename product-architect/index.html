Standard contract

Six files. Every serious product.

The Product Architecture Pack is intentionally small. It gives humans and agents a shared contract without turning product work into document maintenance.

01

Intent

User, painful job, desired outcome, non-goals and the reason this deserves to exist.

02

Product spec

Core workflows, user-visible behaviour, failure states and acceptance criteria.

03

Architecture

Data, APIs, system boundaries, capabilities, trust boundaries and hard-to-reverse decisions.

04

Constraints

Security, privacy, authority, compliance, performance, cost and claim boundaries.

05

Golden cases

Exactly three flagship workflows with realistic inputs, failure conditions and proof requirements.

06

Verification

Tests, E2E, pilot evidence, known gaps and the bar for the next proof level.

How it stays alive

Any PR that changes product behaviour, a trust boundary, a hard-to-reverse architecture choice, a golden case or the evidence behind a public claim updates the relevant pack in the same change. Implementation details that stay inside the contract do not create documentation churn.
