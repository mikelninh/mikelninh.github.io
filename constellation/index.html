THE THROUGH-LINE

Range is only useful when the reason underneath it is clear.

I keep asking variations of the same question: can a complicated thing become easier to understand, safer to act on, or simply more joyful to experience?

01 / RECURRING QUESTIONS

Different projects.
Same instincts.

The technologies change. The questions underneath them are surprisingly stable.

01

Can this be radically simpler?

Rules, documents and workflows should not consume attention just because the system behind them is complicated.

02

Can the human stay in control?

Automation is most useful when evidence, uncertainty and consequential decisions remain visible.

03

Does this deserve attention?

Useful products save effort. Great products can also create delight, connection, curiosity or a reason to return.
