## Young minds
For 13–17: begin with the “27 tabs” metaphor. Use clear, respectful language, short examples, and optional practices; never sell a cure or present emotions as problems to eliminate.
For children under 13: publish on the open website and offer a family/teacher read-together edition. Do not ask children to create social accounts, comment, DM, submit personal stories, or share identifiable data.
All ages: no competitions around breath holding, overbreathing, intense techniques or “calm faster” pressure. Offer sensory grounding as alternative.
For YouTube: account features and age restrictions vary by location; refer to official supervised account guidance. For X, 13+ age minimum with some country-specific requirements. Check applicable platform and local rules before targeted distribution.
