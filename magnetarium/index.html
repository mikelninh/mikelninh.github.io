A NOTE ON THE NATURE OF WONDER

Beauty can invite a question.
Evidence helps answer it.

The magnetic and electrical scenes use simplified models grounded in established physics. The luminous effects, motion trails and architecture studies are visual interpretations. Circular forms are powerful in both mathematics and design—but geometric resemblance alone is not evidence of mysterious energies.
