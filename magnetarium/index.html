A LITTLE BIT OF WONDER

What's really happening?

Six experiments are only the beginning. Here are three clues to carry into the laboratory.

01 / TWO SIDES

Opposites attract.

Every bar magnet has a north and south pole. Bring opposite poles together and the field links up. Put like poles together and watch the pattern bend away.

02 / INVISIBLE GEOMETRY

One field, infinite possibilities.

Magnetic fields have a direction and strength at every point. With multiple magnets, their fields add together — creating wonderfully complex shapes.

03 / REAL VS. RENDERED

Ferrocell magic is light.

Real Ferrocells use an ultra-thin magnetic nanoparticle suspension and carefully placed lights. Our Glow mode is an interpretation, while Field lines and Compass use simplified pole models. Free physics uses a damped, simplified 2D magnetic-force model; Breathe, Orbit and Spin are choreographed movements.
