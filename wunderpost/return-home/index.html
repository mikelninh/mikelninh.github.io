A little death before a wonderful life.

Approx. 3 minutes

A question for you ↘

Before you do anything, die.

Not literally, of course.

Let the version of you that believes it must earn its right to exist die.

The one who wakes up already behind. Who measures the day before it has even begun. Who believes that somewhere, someday, after enough effort, enough healing, enough success, life will finally begin.

Let that person rest.

R.I.P.
Rest in peace.

For a moment, put down everything you think you need to become.

The better body. The greater purpose. The extraordinary future. The person you promised yourself you’d be by now.

Put it all down.

Take a breath.

Feel the ground beneath you. Notice the sounds around you. The quiet miracle of being here, without having to justify it.

Perhaps this is what it means to return home.

Not to a house, a country, or even another person.

But to yourself.

To the simple experience of being alive, before the world tells you what to do with that life.

We spend so much time doing.

Building. Improving. Learning. Achieving. Becoming.

Even our rest becomes another task to complete. Our meditation another performance. Our happiness another goal.

And none of these things are wrong.

There is beauty in creation, ambition, and effort.

But when we act from the belief that we are not enough, even our greatest achievements may leave us searching for something more.

So what if we reversed the order?

Before doing, become.
And before becoming, simply be.

Let the noise settle.

Let the imaginary audience disappear.

Let the person you were trying so hard to prove yourself to rest in peace.

Then begin.

Make breakfast. Call someone you love. Write the story. Build something wonderful. Stand up for someone who needs you.

Not because your existence requires justification.

But because being alive is an opportunity to give something beautiful to the world.

You don't need to abandon your dreams.

Just stop abandoning yourself on the way towards them.

Return home first.

And from there, begin again.

Rest in peace.
Then live.

The world can
wait a moment.
Welcome home.
One question to take with you

What would you do today if you had nothing to prove?

Your answer doesn't need to be public. Write it for yourself, copy it, keep it, or leave it here and carry the question with you.

The world can wait a moment. Welcome home.
