Future memory · written in 2026 · spoken from 2083

To my next 90 years.

A speech from my 90th birthday — about the future we created, the enlightenment that never stayed forever, and the love that made the whole journey worthwhile.

This began as a coaching exercise with my friend Diana: look backwards from the end of a long life and ask what was truly worth building, becoming and giving.

01 / Hey, fam

Ninety used to mean old.

Hey fam.

I just came back from meditation, the sauna and a little travelling with my beloved. It is strange to remember that ninety once meant very old — sick, fragile, almost gone.

But I feel fit. I feel curious. And I am genuinely looking forward to the next ninety years.

The best is yet to be.

02 / The honest spiritual report

I never managed to stay enlightened forever.

I am still a little confused.

That experience of finally being awake — fully present, peaceful, one with everything — came many times. But it never became the permanent state I once imagined.

It came and went. There were openings and contractions. Bliss and fear. Clarity and old habits.

Every day.
Every week.
Every month.
Every year.
Every decade.

Perhaps awakening was never a trophy to keep. Perhaps it was the gradual realisation that nothing had ever been outside the sacred — not even the confusion.

03 / Looking backwards

We built what once sounded impossible.

There was a time when humanity believed disease, hunger, poverty, borders and ecological destruction were simply part of reality.

Now, many children learn about them the way we once learned about ancient plagues: as terrible things people endured before we knew how to organise intelligence, energy and care differently.

Preventive medicine and healthy lives far beyond 120
Zero hunger and extreme poverty
A largely vegan food system
Renewable energy and green cities
Autonomous production of essential goods
Families living across generations
Project-based work and new forms of ownership
Research on Earth, moons and other planets
Human and AI intelligence growing together
World peace becoming ordinary enough to forget it was once called naïve

The technology mattered. But the deeper breakthrough was learning that abundance without belonging still feels empty, and intelligence without compassion merely scales confusion.

04 / Home

My dream was never only a machine.

It was a place to call our own.

A huge family. Gardens and food growing around us. A kitchen filled with people. A sauna, a gym, a cinema and quiet rooms for practice. Children, elders and friends sharing one living ecosystem without losing their freedom.

A safe space without shame. A place where nobody had to perform perfection to belong.

We travelled through cities and beyond Earth. Yet some of the most important journeys were still from one human being to another.

05 / The wave

What I would tell every child.

You are a wave. 🌊I am a wave. Trees, dogs and stars are waves.

A wave may believe it is separate, yet it is always the whole ocean moving in one temporary shape.

Atman and Brahman. The individual and the whole. Self, God, the One — different names pointing towards the same impossible intimacy.

I would tell them:

I am calm.
I am kind.
I am part of everything.
06 / What love became

Love was the only purpose large enough.

Not love as possession. Not love as being endlessly agreeable. Not love as a beautiful word placed over fear.

True love meant seeing the whole person. Protecting freedom without abandoning responsibility. Creating enough space for another being to become more fully themselves.

It meant making our life a gift to the whole world — not because we were pure, but because the same divine spark lived in every creature.

Even if it took my whole life to understand that, this was my whole life.

07 / Testament

In case I die today.

Do not wait for the perfect future to begin living from it.

Create space. Create magic. Create love. Create bliss where you can.

Learn. Grow. Heal. Build things of true quality — objects, systems, relationships and moments that carry care all the way through them.

“Waste no more time arguing what a good person should be. Be one.”

Your thoughts are clouds. Some are bright, some are storms. But you are also the sky: vast enough to hold them without becoming any single one.

08 / We already won

Infinity was hiding inside the day.

What is a day? Four years? Fifteen years? Ninety?

From one perspective, they disappear instantly. From another, one moment of depth contains everything.

I spent much of my life looking for the next challenge, the next transformation, the next horizon beyond the horizon.

Stop.
We already won.

The experience of oneness was never elsewhere. Bliss was not waiting at the end of history. God was not a prize awarded to a perfected version of me.

Life was the ride. We were space travelling all along.

The future is not a place we are going. It is something we are creating.

To the next 90.
Michael Ninh · Future Memory, 2083
