STANDARD CHESS // PATTERN TRAINER

Italian Game

01

Board geometry

Three fixed 4×4 boards sit at elevations 2, 4 and 6. Four 2×2 attack boards can move between corner posts, giving 64 playable squares in total.

02

Piece movement

Pieces retain ordinary chess movement in the top-down projection, but may finish on another elevation. Sliding pieces follow the highest available path between start and target.

03

Attack boards

Moving an owned attack board replaces a piece move. It must be empty or carry only one of your pawns, and it may translate one or two ranks along its file and/or invert.

04

Ownership

If you capture the final opposing piece on an enemy attack board, ownership transfers to you. Empty boards retain their previous owner.

05

Pawns

Pawns promote on ranks 8/9 for White and 1/0 for Black. The optional rook-pawn rule lets pawns stranded on the a/f files move inward.

06

Engine coverage

Implemented: main/attack geometry, projected movement, Path A/Path B, captures, king-safety validation, promotion, attack-board movement/inversion/ownership and rook-pawn option. Not yet: the rare Tri-D castling and en-passant edge cases.
