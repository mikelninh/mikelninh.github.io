Customer-side approval

Legal disputes, safety incidents, financial instructions and contractor commitments are escalated to the customer's nominated decision-maker.

≋
Every step evidenced

Policy version, actor, action, timestamp and linked receipt. The demo builds a simple hash chain; production needs stronger guarantees.

No AI may promote its own authority. In the backend, money movement, access grants, legal commitments and destruction of data are denied or require explicit tenant authorisation.

THE REAL RELEASE GATE

Build trust before autonomy.

Production means real credentials, lawful processing, reliable infrastructure and observed results—not merely a persuasive interface.

⌁
Data boundaries

Secure tenant isolation, signed webhooks, EU-hosting review, GDPR documentation and a deletion/retention policy before real tenant data.

⏱
Measured outcomes

Compare handling time and correctness against a genuine baseline. Unknown saving estimates remain unknown.

◇
Fail closed

When an action fails or authority is unclear, create a visible ticket for the customer's manager; never manufacture completion.
