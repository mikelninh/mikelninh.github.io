THE QUESTION

Can a digital collectible have presence?

I wanted the card to respond like an object you are holding, not a flat asset sitting inside a page.

THE BUILD

Depth without gimmicks.

Layered depth, pointer and motion input, realtime light and a material system tuned around the artwork rather than pasted on top of it.

WHY IT'S HERE

Engineering can have taste.

This is where I play with interaction, worldbuilding and visual systems without needing the experiment to justify itself as enterprise software.
