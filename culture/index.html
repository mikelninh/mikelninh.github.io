02 / What stays

The recurring signals.

The works change. These patterns keep showing up — probably because they are also the questions underneath my own work.

Connection

Can two inner worlds really meet?

I love stories that treat attention, honesty and presence as real forms of action.

Time

Who might we have become?

Memory, alternate lives and small choices that quietly bend an entire future.

Beauty

Can form carry truth?

Colour, rhythm, sound, silence and interfaces that make an idea felt before it is explained.

03 / Interactive worlds

Play is serious research.

Games taught me systems thinking before I had a name for it: rules, feedback loops, emergence, mastery, surprise and the joy of trying one more run.

Games that made worlds feel possible

Adventure, elegant mechanics and the peculiar magic of a few pixels becoming a place you remember for life.

Non-duality & awareness

Different traditions, one recurring invitation: investigate experience directly and notice what remains before the labels arrive.
