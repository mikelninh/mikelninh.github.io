# GOOD THINGS · Season One — Five little tastes
Release: 2.7 · 16 October 2026

## Product experience

**A category is a pool, not a chore.** An episode draws just five distinct products from its pool, then stops. No "keep spinning forever" requirement. Rated and Not tried answers continue to save on the existing shelf; this local episode session is an optional way to organise a quick round. The page includes themes, available source-photo candidates, progress 1/5–5/5, episode finale, replay and explicit sharing.

The category library has six visible starter topics and six expandable extras. People can always select Freestyle to return to normal random discovery. A user never needs to rate all 45 plant milks to finish Milk Wars.

Episode mode works now in Solo and same-device Together. Two separate logged-in devices still support existing secret votes and brand-only filtering; *synchronised five-episode sessions across two account devices need backend room metadata and a real phone-to-phone test*. Don't claim the private local ratings represent the signed-in Mikel or Huong.

## Two related formats (not the same event)

### Five in Five (3–5 min total)
For *products the hosts already know*. 5 quick packshot-reveal cards; each independently chooses S–F or Not tried; one optional text note; episode recap with favourite and agreement/disagreement. The category pool is larger than the five draws, but the session is deliberately small.

### The Kitchen Table (7–12 min video)
For *real side-by-side tasting*. Buy 3–5 specific SKU variants first, disclose actual preparation, total pricing and package size, then blind-label samples A–E. Each person scores taste, texture and value independently; reveal brands only after scores are locked. This video is not the same as the random generator, and the app should eventually support building a fixed private tasting lineup/shopping list.

### Shelf Safari (short field trip)
In-person store exploration of Vemondo or Food for Future; check actual stock, ingredient lists, Nutri-Score, price label and exact barcodes. Never claim that a product shown in a historical official listing is available at every location.

## A repeatable episode storytelling template

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:10)** — the funniest disagreement, no manufacturer spoilers or misattributed quote.
2. **Premise (0:10–0:25)** — "Five vegan nuggets. Two questionable experts. One Golden Fork."
3. **Pack reveal (0:25–0:40)** — show the real product, current price/100 g if verified, relevant nutrition, preparation method.
4. **Actual tasting (0:40 onward)** — compare honestly, separate personal taste from nutrition and current purchase availability.
5. **Secret judgement / reveal** — Mikel & Huong's independent S–F votes; record any Not tried honestly.
6. **Better, not just worse** — one actionable product-team suggestion: e.g. crispier coating after oven baking, lower saturated fat with equal mouthfeel, less excessive packaging.
7. **Mini finale** — the episode favourite, biggest disagreement and a shareable link inviting friends to make their own choices.

## Production guardrails

- Prefer like-for-like comparisons. Don't judge plant milks as a cheese-melting test.
- Blind tasting should keep preparation as equivalent as possible, with differences disclosed.
- Don't invent current price, stock, ingredients, Nutri-Score, nutrition, sampling or scores.
- Full product packshots need correct EAN/retailer SKU, source provenance, and rights checks before redistribution.
- Non-sponsored verdicts only; disclose gifts or any later brand partnerships.
- Ask friends for independent ratings; keep private opinions private until explicitly shared.
- A single five-product session is **not a representative consumer survey**.
