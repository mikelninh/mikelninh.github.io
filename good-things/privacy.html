Last updated 14 October 2026 · Duo beta · Beta privacy summary. This is an informational disclosure; a complete Germany-specific privacy policy and legal notice must be reviewed and published before active public acquisition.

What this beta stores

Product improvement notes: GOOD THINGS now also stores optional personal taste/texture/value scores and improvement suggestions in localStorage (goodthings_sensory_v26). These are not automatically uploaded, made public, or sent to manufacturers. Only reviews you explicitly allow are included in a manually copied/exported report. Ratings associated with logged-in accounts continue to use the separate ratings service.

Guest mode: your ratings and temporary photo matches are stored in the browser's localStorage on your device, under goodthings_v13_guest and goodthings_v13_images. Duo demo rounds and independently chosen ratings are kept under goodthings_duo_v18. Solo Shuffle progress (current product and product IDs you have rated) is saved locally under goodthings_solo_v20; ratings and your tasting note remain in your local shelf or in your connected account. Product photo-resolution audit results are kept under goodthings_photo_audit_v18 on your own device. They are not sent to our backend. Frozen guest share links intentionally include only product identifiers and S–D scores, not your personal tasting notes.

With an account: Supabase Auth stores your login email and credentials; our own app stores your public handle, display name, optional bio, private or public product ratings, notes, lists, follow relationships, privately submitted Duo votes, shared Duo room membership, conversation notes submitted only after reveal, and reveal history, and private feedback and any photo-correction report you choose to submit (reason, suggested source link and note). Photo corrections are visible only to the reporting account and project administrators; submission never publishes an image automatically. All data access uses row-level security rules. You can edit or remove individual ratings and sign out.

Public by design: your public display name, username and any ratings, notes, lists or follows you choose to publish are viewable by other visitors. New product ratings default to private. Votes cast in a Duo room are not disclosed to the second participant until both participants have answered. A Duo room URL only works for its two authorized accounts. If sharing a physical browser in demo mode, both votes exist on that device. Never enter sensitive personal data into your public bio or reviews.

Services involved

Website hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Database and authentication: Supabase, project region EU Central (Frankfurt). Account services may transfer some operational metadata outside the EU under provider terms; verify provider DPA before broad launch. Fonts and scripts: Google Fonts and jsDelivr, loaded for page typography and the browser database client. Product images: the page loads referenced packshots from retailer, manufacturer and product-community CDNs (including REWE, Knuspr, vly and others). Those hosts may receive technical request data when pictures load. Product details may retrieve barcode-specific nutrition from Open Food Facts and historical shop price observations from Open Prices when you open an item. Both services are operated separately from GOOD THINGS and may receive your technical request data (including IP address). A candidate photo search is started only when you explicitly press that control. The catalogued barcode images and nutrition information originate from community contributions under the Open Food Facts ODbL licence. Retailer product links open their sites only on click.

This beta does not embed Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or ad tracking. The providers named above may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, in order to serve their resources.

Your options

Use the shelf without creating an account. Export your ratings as JSON. Delete individual ratings in your profile. Clear the localStorage entries above to delete guest device data. If you need access to or deletion of account records, contact the project owner through the GitHub project issue tracker without posting private data publicly. A dedicated private deletion/contact channel is still required for production use.

Operator: Michael Ninh (GOOD THINGS — Mikel & Huong). The legally required full provider identification and a private GDPR contact address are not yet published and must be completed before inviting the general public to register.

Honesty is a feature.

This is a community beta. We won't present an unverified product image as proof, or private ratings as public opinions.
