## Review checklist

- Exact brand, flavour, package size and barcode; no lookalike substitutions
- Product visible in full (not cropped top/bottom), minimum 450 px on both axes for Duo
- Source/reuse rights and CDN behaviour recorded
- Human variant review before marking photo as fully approved
- Four similarly named SKU pairs still need deduplication assessment, keeping legacy ratings intact
