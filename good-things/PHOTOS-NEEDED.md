# GOOD THINGS · Photo Integrity Ledger · 15 October 2026

**323/354** rankable catalogue products have source-linked packshots. **31** still lack an exact product image. The linked-image count does **not** mean that all sources are currently reachable, pixel-sharp, properly licensed for re-use, or visually checked against today's physical packaging.

## Required release gate

1. Confirm that the product actually exists, with manufacturer, precise variety/size and GTIN or unambiguous official retailer SKU.
2. Obtain a rights-appropriate exact front-pack image from Open Food Facts, official manufacturer or retailer. Never substitute an adjacent flavour or alternative product.
3. Verify the image URL loads in the browser, has enough native resolution for the viewport, and shows the complete pack without crops.
4. Verify the front text/variety/quantity visually against the source and store provenance on the item. When there is doubt, leave the item in review.
5. Record `photo_match_method`, `image_source_url` and `photo_reviewed_at` on the catalogue record; preserve historic ratings and product identity.

## Status definitions

- **missing:** no exact packshot source; no arbitrary photo fallback.
- **linked / unchecked:** photo URL is associated with source but untested in current browser.
- **low / broken:** URL fails or packshot resolution insufficient.
- **good resolution:** image loaded at full scale. This does *not* mean variant identity confirmed.
- **verified:** source, barcode/SKU, image, size and variant human checked and legally safe for display.
