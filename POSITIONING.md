## Main portfolio proof trio

The main hiring path should feature no more than these three proof families unless evidence materially changes:

1. **TrustReady** — bounded agent autonomy, evidence, human authority and production/promotion gates.
2. **CivicOS / SafeTrace Entity Resolution** — evidence-first investigation plus measurable entity-resolution evaluation and calibrated abstention.
3. **DRV SignalLab** — deterministic public-sector monitoring, statistical reasoning, uncertainty and human-review boundaries.

PrüfPilot, MissionOps, CasePilot and other projects remain valuable targeted application proofs. They should not crowd the main hiring path.

## Claim discipline

- Do not describe synthetic evaluation as production performance.
- Do not turn developer-authored benchmarks into independent validation.
- Do not claim legal, compliance, public-sector or security authority that the project does not possess.
- Prefer a concrete metric, executable gate or inspectable trace over adjectives.
- Keep explicit `unknown`, `review`, `not yet` and human-approval states when the evidence requires them.

## Hiring-path acceptance test

Within 60 seconds, a reviewer should be able to answer:

1. What does Michael build?
2. Why is his use of coding agents more rigorous than simple code generation?
3. What is one measurable proof?
4. Where does human authority remain?
5. What should I click next?
