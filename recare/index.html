Why this problem

I see the cost of bad clinical software after work, not just in architecture diagrams.

My partner is a hospital physician in Berlin. When fragmented systems and documentation eat her evening, the product metric becomes painfully concrete: good software should return that time to patients — and to her life.

That motivated CareOS: an independent reference architecture for trustworthy clinical context. This page narrows it to one hiring-relevant question: can an agent prepare useful documentation while preserving provenance, missingness, contradictions and human control?

04 · Engineering boundary

The model is a proposer, not the authority.

The transferable pattern behind CareOS and my other agent projects is simple: keep identity, permissions, source truth, evidence requirements and consequential actions outside model discretion.
