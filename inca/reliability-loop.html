What I actually built

A small safety layer—not another claims agent.

It checks the evidence an agent used, explains what failed and prevents a flawed trace from moving forward unexamined.

InputRecorded trace + policy files

Case metadata, cited document, exact quotation, tool sequence and requested action.

CheckSix executable rules

Confirm applicable sources and quotations, safe tool order, authorised actions and bounded next steps.

OutputBlock, explain, retest

Unsafe examples are held for human review. Saved corrections are checked against repeat failures.
