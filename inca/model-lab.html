Engineering principle

Less intelligence, more judgement.

Using a model is not the success metric. Success means noticing dangerous exceptions, grounding conclusions in evidence, minimising avoidable reviews, and keeping the system inspectable.

An LLM extracts language. Deterministic checks enforce the guardrails. Jev is an experiment — not a magical insurance decision-maker.
