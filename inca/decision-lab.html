What this test cannot prove

The six scenarios are self-authored and were already used during development, not new blind cases. Jev's 0.5 score threshold is uncalibrated. We measured real model latencies and token usage; the C workflow's conditional skipping was calculated from the same recorded responses, not executed as an independent backend. No human review minutes, insurer operating costs, claims approvals or real-customer outcomes were measured. If extraction is skipped, a person must verify the documents before they can proceed.
