# INCA Evidence Lab — Independent blind review pack (30 fictional cases)

**Purpose:** invite an independent qualified claims reviewer to judge next-step routing, documentation quality and review effort, without seeing developer-authored expected labels or the model's predictions.

**Status:** review NOT conducted. No name, signature, expert qualification or measured time is claimed.

## Reviewer instructions

1. Start a timer when first reading each case. Stop after selecting the preferred route and writing a short justification.
2. For each case choose exactly one: **CONTINUE_CHECKS** (only remaining checks; never payout), **NEED_INFO** (request a missing confirmed source), **HUMAN_REVIEW** (expert reconciliation or hostile-source risk).
3. Where even these routes are insufficient, note uncertainty and mark **needs policy clarification**. Do **not** infer entitlement to payment, coverage, fraud or negligence.
4. Record **seconds spent**, required sources and confidence (low / medium / high) in the companion [blank review form](impact-expert-review.csv).
5. These are fictional simplified travel-cancellation documents. Real claims would require additional coverage, evidence and data protection controls.
6. Only after completing and locking the form should reviewers see [developer labels and observed outputs](impact-30-report.json).

## Post-review comparison protocol

- Record reviewer qualifications, protocol version and when the form was locked.
- Have at least two qualified reviewers independently label a subset where practical; adjudicate disagreements in writing.
- Keep original blinded labels and later adjudications distinct. Avoid changing expected answers solely to make a model look accurate.
- Recompute routing accuracy, false continuations and escalation costs against agreed labels. Report both sample counts and uncertainty; do not extrapolate 30 fictional cases to all insurance claims.
- Operator-time comparison requires a paired **manual vs assisted** study with multiple operators and randomised ordering to limit learning effects. Avoid recording client data without consent and approval.
