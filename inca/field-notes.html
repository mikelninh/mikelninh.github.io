05 · An engineering boundary

AI suggests. Evidence and people decide.

I would automate

Structured evidence gathering, source checks, missing-information drafts, failure replay, safe specialist routing, operational dashboards and regression tests.

I would not automate without explicit authority

Coverage determinations, liability acceptance, payments, consequential customer communications, or silent decisions based on unverified model confidence.

This is a portfolio experiment using wholly fictional documents and code executed in a controlled environment. The model comparisons are exploratory, developer-authored experiments; they are not expert-reviewed real-claims benchmarks or evidence of production reliability. AI tools helped me build and test this work; I remain accountable for the approach, checks, interpretations and what is presented here.
