05 / Research integrity

What we did not prove.

This is developer-authored fiction, not an insurer pilot.

Many planted trace defects are structural, so fixed rules can legitimately outperform AI. All 300 cases came from the same generator even though the development and holdout document templates were different. A decision score is not evidence or claim approval. Human review costs and end-to-end claim times have not been measured, and neither provider is validated on real customer files.

The original 300-case experiment used Jev 1.13.0. A separate real, 160-call bilingual version test compared pinned Jev 1.13.0 and 1.14.0. Neither version demonstrated a quality advantage on its authored cases; this does not establish general model superiority.
